About Campus Canteen

Campus Canteen has been serving students and staff since day one — from quick breakfast bites before an 8 AM lecture to a full thali after a long lab session. We cook everything fresh, every morning, using ingredients sourced daily.

  • Hygienic kitchen — FSSAI-certified staff and daily sanitation checks.
  • Student-friendly prices — Most meals under ₹80, because hostel budgets are real.
  • Fast service — Order ahead online and skip the lunch-rush queue entirely.
  • Something for everyone — Veg, non-veg, and a growing list of healthy options.
